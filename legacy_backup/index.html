About the Chandigarh Birding Club

We are a community of passionate birdwatchers exploring the rich avifauna of Chandigarh and the Shivalik foothills. From the wetlands of Sukhna Lake to the dense canopies of the Morni Hills, we document, protect, and celebrate every feathered encounter.

🦅
🌅

Regular Dawn Walks

Weekly early-morning outings to prime birding spots around the city and Shivalik range.

📔

Bird Census

Participate in seasonal bird counts and contribute to national databases like eBird India.

🔭

Optics Sharing

Binoculars and spotting scopes available for new members during club walks.

Conservation

We work with local authorities to protect nesting sites and habitats across Chandigarh.

📸

Photography Mentoring

Experienced bird photographers guide beginners in ethical wildlife photography.

🏫

School Outreach

We visit schools to spark curiosity about birds and nature in the next generation.
